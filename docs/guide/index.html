How your team runs its week in NicoDesk

One place for who owns what, what is due, and what changed. Each page below shows a working model of a screen. Press the buttons, they react the way the app does.

Start here

Sign in with your work Google account. Then the sidebar on the left takes you everywhere.

  1. Open nico-desk.vercel.app and press Continue with Google.
  2. Pick your work account. You get in only if you are on the team roster.
  3. If you see "That account is not on the team roster", ask the team admin to add you.

Add and work a task

Press n or + New task from anywhere. Type one sentence, and Quick Add fills in the fields.

This page reads simple sentences only, to show the idea. In the app, you check every field before you press Add task. You can also open Fill It In Yourself and type each field.

The fields

  • Task: what will be done. Owner: one person. Due Date: today or later.
  • Project: pick one, or "None (one-off task)". Note and Details are optional.

Work a task

  1. Click a task row. A side panel opens with every field, the history and Copy link.
  2. Change the status, the owner or the note, then press Save changes.
  3. To add a Doc, Sheet or Figma link, paste it in Paste a link and press Add link.
  4. Press Open full page to add an update to the log or to set priority (leads).
The first due date is keptA due date can move, but you must write a reason (10 to 280 characters). The app counts each push and shows the earlier date struck out.
Undo is a new changeAfter a status, date or edit change, a toast offers Undo. Undo records a new change. The history is never erased.

Who can do what with a task

An admin has every right a lead has. A person a blocked task waits on can also unblock it, and writes what they did.

  • Dropped, and a move back to Not started, also ask for a reason.

Turn on help mode with ?. Then hover a dotted word in the app to read what it means.

Projects

A project holds its tasks, its people and its date. Anyone on the team can start one from + New project.

  1. Go to Projects and press + New project.
  2. Fill in Project Name, Internal or Partner / Collaboration, and a Target Date with its label (Launch, Event, Deadline).
  3. Set the Project Owner, the Project Lead and the people. Add sub-projects as chips.
  4. Press Start project. Use the Set up this project list to finish.

Turn meeting minutes into tasks

Paste the notes from a meeting. The app reads the next steps and proposes tasks. Nothing changes until a person accepts.

Festive capsule › Import minutes

Where the minutes can come from

  • Paste: any notes. Set the meeting date and name, then press Read minutes.
  • Granola, Fireflies, Fathom: search your meetings and pick one. Each needs its key set up first. On the hosted app, ask the repo owner.
  • Wispr Flow: copy its summary and use Paste.
  • Google Doc and Google Meet: Not on yet
A person decidesEach accepted step becomes a task with an owner and a date. A step with no owner or date shows "Needs owner and date". The same meeting cannot be imported twice.

Reminders

For leads. The app writes one reminder per person. It opens as a draft in your own Gmail. You read it and press Send. The app never sends.

  1. Go to More › Reminders, or press Reminders on a board.
  2. Pick Overdue only or Overdue + due soon, and a project. Press Apply.
  3. Untick any item you do not want to chase.
  4. Press Open in Gmail. Check the draft, then send it yourself.

"reminded 2 days ago" shows when you last chased that person. Every day at 09:00 the app also puts due and late notices on Home. It sends no email.

Feedback

Tell us what works and what does not. Press Feedback in the top bar.

  1. Write your note. Add the page and up to three screenshots: paste, drop or press + Add screenshot.
  2. Press Send. Every note on the team appears in the list, newest first.
  3. Agree with a note? Press +1, I agree. One vote per person.
  4. When someone replies, you see it on Home under "Replies to your feedback".

Roster and roles

Everyone can see the roster under More › Roster. Only an admin can change it.

MemberAdds and works their own tasks, starts projects, imports minutes, sends feedback.
LeadAlso reassigns, reopens, sets priority, drafts reminders and sees everyone's Ledger.
AdminEverything a lead can do. Also changes the roster, uses View as, and deletes archived projects.

Add people (admin)

  1. One person: fill in Name, Job title, Department and Email. Tick Lead or Admin if needed. Press Add to roster.
  2. A whole team: in Add several people, paste one person per line as name, job title, department, email. Press Add everyone.
  3. Press Copy invite and send the link to them yourself.
Sam Rao, Designer, Brand, sam@example.com
Ira Shah, Merchandiser, Product, ira@example.com

Each line needs all four parts. Everyone added this way starts as a member.

View as is read-onlyAn admin can see the app as another person: account menu › View as someone else. Nothing can be changed in this mode. Press Switch back to you to leave.

The Ledger

More › Ledger shows weekly delivery numbers. Click a number to see its tasks. Leads and admins see everyone. A member sees only their own.

Look and phone

Open the account menu at the foot of the sidebar.

  • Appearance: two skins, Warm (the default) and Cool, each in Light or Dark.
  • Your profile: your name, a nickname, a phone number and a photo. Your photo shows across the app.
  • Guide opens the short guide inside the app. Keyboard shortcuts lists the keys.

Put it on your phone

  1. Open the app in your phone's browser and sign in.
  2. iPhone: Share › Add to Home Screen. Android: menu › Install app.
  3. It opens full screen, like an app. The mic button in task fields uses your phone's dictation.
