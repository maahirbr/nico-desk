The run

0:0030 seconds · the problem

Why this exists

  • Today our commitments live in Sheets, meeting notes and WhatsApp.
  • Leads chase people for status, and dates slip without anyone saying so.
  • Nico Desk is one place where a team runs its week. Every task has one owner and one date.
  1. Show the sign-in page, then sign in with Google. You land on Home.

Land this: only people on the team list, with a company Google account, get in.

0:3060 seconds · Home

What I owe, on one page

  • This is Home. Everything I owe, across every project, sorted by date.
  • Late and at risk are worked out from the dates. Nobody picks a colour.
  • Changing a status is one click, and the toast offers Undo for a few seconds.
  1. Point at the due dates. On Team, late and at-risk tasks carry a Late or At risk flag.
  2. Open the status menu on a row. Pick In progress.
  3. Point at the toast with Undo.

Land this: no one has to ask "what is on my plate". It also works on a phone, no app to install.

1:3060 seconds · a slip, in the open

A date moves with a reason, never quietly

  • Say I cannot make this date. I move it, and I have to say why.
  • The first date stays on record. The ledger judges on-time against it.
  • If I am stuck on someone, I mark it Blocked and name who I am waiting on.
  1. Open the demo task. Show the update log.
  2. Move date…, pick a new date, type a reason, save.
  3. Status menu, Blocked. Type the reason and pick the teammate.

Land this: a late task now has a new date and a reason, logged before the old date passed.

2:3045 seconds · it reaches the right people

The people involved hear about it

  • The project lead sees the date move and the block under "Updates for you".
  • The teammate I am waiting on sees it on their own Home, under "Waiting on you".
  • When they are done, they say what they did, and the block clears. Nobody chased anyone.
  1. Account menu, View as someone else, pick the teammate. Show Waiting on you.
  2. Switch back. Show Updates for you on the lead's Home.

Land this: View as is read only. You see their screen and nothing is written as them.

3:1545 seconds · the lead's week

The weekly review, without a round of messages

  • The team page shows everyone's open work, by person or by status.
  • Each project has its own board, with its own people.
  • Adding a task is one line: what, when, who and which project.
  1. Open Team, then By status. Point at Blocked.
  2. Open a project from Projects.
  3. Press n and type a quick-add line, such as Send deck to vendor fri @name #project.

Land this: a task in a project goes only to that project's people.

4:0035 seconds · the record

Who closed on time, and what needs attention

  • The ledger shows, per person, how many dates were kept, against the date first given.
  • A date moved before it passed is fine. A date that slips with no word is "slipped silently", and the target is zero.
  • Below, Needs attention lists each late task, why, and the next step.
  • Reminders open a draft in my own Gmail. The app never sends anything. I press Send.
  1. Open Ledger from More. Point at one person card, then scroll to Needs attention.
  2. Open Reminders. Read the line under the title aloud.
4:3525 seconds · close

What happens next

  • The pilot team uses it every day from now. New to-dos go here, not in a sheet.
  • We review what blocked people on Wednesday 14 October and fix it.
  • If the first days hold, a second team starts on Monday 19 October.

If they ask

Does it email or message people for me?
No. Reminders open a draft in your own Gmail, from your address, and you press Send. Inside the app, people see notices on their Home.
Who can sign in?
Only people on the team list, with a Google account on the company domain. A few named outside addresses can be allowed one by one.
Who can change my task?
You, the project's owner or lead, team leads and admins. Admins can also view anyone's screens, read only.
What if I cannot make a date?
Move it before it passes and give a reason. The ledger counts that as a moved date, not a silent slip.
How do I say I am stuck on someone?
Mark the task Blocked and name the teammate. It shows on their Home under "Waiting on you", and they clear it with a note.
Can it read our meeting notes?
Yes, per project: Import minutes, paste the notes or pull them from Granola or Fireflies, and accept the tasks it proposes. Reading every note-taker on its own comes after the pilot.
Priorities? Locked due dates?
Not yet. Both are on the list after the first review.
Where is the data?
In a database in Mumbai. Only the app reads it, and only after a sign-in.
Why can I not change some tasks?
Tasks read from a Sheet stay read only, so the Sheet and the desk never disagree. Change them in the Sheet.
Is there an app?
It runs in the browser on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install.

If something breaks

The site will not load

Present from this page. Each beat has its screens. Click a screenshot to open it full size.

A page shows an error

Reload once. If it stays, go to the next beat and say you will follow up. Note the page and the step.

Sign-in fails for you

Use "Use another account" in Google and pick the work account. If that fails, present from this page.

You run long

Cut the project board and quick add in beat five. Keep the slip, the block and the ledger: they carry the point.
